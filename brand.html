Mark

Logo

Two lockups: the full wordmark for headers and the logomark when space is tight. Prefer white on black. Use the black versions only on light backgrounds.

Clear space

Keep clear space around the mark equal to at least half the logomark height on every side. Don’t crowd it with type, photos, or other logos.

Usage

Do’s & don’ts

The mark is a signal, not decoration. If it starts to look like a sticker pack, dial it back.

Do

Use the official files

Download the SVG kit. Don’t redraw, trace, or rebuild the letterforms.

Don’t

Stretch or squash

Scale proportionally. Distorted marks look cheap and break the wordmark.

Do

Keep contrast high

White on black, or black on light. The mark should read instantly at a glance.

Don’t

Recolor the mark

Don’t tint it Pulse green, Flash yellow, or Base orange. Color belongs to the product lineup.

Do

Pick one lockup

Use the wordmark or the logomark — not both stacked in the same corner.

Don’t

Rotate, outline, or drop shadow

No glow, bevel, stroke, or 3D. Flat mark, clear background, done.

Palette

Color

Black and white carry the brand. Pulse, Flash, and Base only appear with their product. Don’t use product colors as general accent chrome.

Typography

Type

Display is Moon Walk (renders all-caps). Body and UI are Inter. Headlines stay short. Body stays readable.

AMPL

Moon Walk · Display / Hero

Stay Loud. Stay Healthy. Mix it late. Keep the morning yours. Functional support for nights that go loud — without dimming the morning.

Inter Regular · Body / Default

Do

  • Use Moon Walk for brand lines and section titles
  • Keep display lines to a few words
  • Set body in Inter at comfortable reading sizes

Don’t

  • Pair random display fonts with the AMPL mark
  • Set long paragraphs in Moon Walk
  • Force title case — Moon Walk already reads as caps

Copy

Voice

Short. Direct. Night-out energy with a clean recovery. No wellness lecture.

Line

Stay Loud. Stay Healthy.

Primary lockup line. Use it as a close, a cover, or a header — not as body copy.

Promise

Protect the morning.

Enjoy the moment, protect your morning. Mix one sachet into 650–700 ml when the night winds down.

Do

  • Sound confident and brief
  • Talk about nights and mornings in the same breath
  • Lead with the ritual, not ingredient jargon

Don’t

  • Promise medical outcomes or hangover cures
  • Sound like a spa brochure or a gym bro rant
  • Overexplain — if it needs a footnote, cut it

Lineup

Three modes. One ritual.

Flavor color is the only place the palette gets loud. Don’t mix Pulse green into Base art, or Flash yellow into Pulse.

Charged for Endurance

Pulse

Steady recovery for long nights and louder rooms. Green stays with Pulse.

Primed for Power

Flash

Fast clarity for nights that push past the usual finish line. Yellow stays with Flash.

Tuned for Recovery

Base

Everyday support when you want balance, not a reset button. Orange stays with Base.
